## New

- Photos, videos and music now open in a full-screen viewer. Swipe or use the buttons to go to the previous, next, first or last item in the list you are browsing. Pinch or double-tap to zoom photos; the ⋮ menu has Download, Share, Open with and the other file actions.

## Fixes

- "Last backup" now shows the time of your last successful backup, and it is kept when you close and reopen the app.
- Notices about storage, folders and read-only Backups can be closed with an X and stay hidden.

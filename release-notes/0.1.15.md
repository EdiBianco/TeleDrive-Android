## What's new

- Faster, smoother previews. Photos and videos show a quick preview first, then a sharper one when the cards are large.
- Previews for photos you back up from this phone are made on the phone, so they appear without downloading anything.
- Scrolling through a big library is lighter. Only the cards on screen are drawn, and previews for cards you scroll past are skipped.
- About now shows how many previews loaded, and the last problem if one failed.

## Fixes

- Files with no preview are remembered, so the app stops asking for them every time.
- Previews pause when Telegram asks the app to slow down, then continue.
